Where the concept comes from

Pulse grew out of the Kamerton case: a transformer venue lives by changeovers, and managing them in spreadsheets and chats is risky.

risk: schedule conflicts risk: lost rigging statuses risk: crew misalignment

The risks are design assumptions; no venue interviews were conducted. Hall parameters come from the Kamerton competition project (case).
