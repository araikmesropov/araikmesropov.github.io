Approach

Don’t guess – verify. Every option splits into facts, fears and unverified guesses.

facts fears guesses

What’s actually verified is usually less than it seems. Vetka helps you gather real data about your options – in small steps.

Trunk

As things are now

The baseline: every option’s deviation is measured from it.

Branches

The options

Two is enough, a third shows up on its own. Each branch has a benefit, a risk and a confidence.

Confidence

Facts vs guesses

It rises when guesses turn into facts, not when you just think about it longer.

The branch metaphor: the trunk is the present, branches are options; the choice grows from verified facts

A reversible step
decision→ branch map→ experiment · 5 evenings→ daily note→ outcome: confidence

Instead of a “yes” or “no” – a short experiment over a few days. You come back from it with facts.
