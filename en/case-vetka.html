Approach

Don’t guess – verify. Every option splits into facts, fears and unverified guesses.

facts fears guesses

What’s actually verified is usually less than it seems. Vetka helps you gather real data about your options – in small steps.

Research.

5 users · prototype and a conversation
How it ran

I handed over the prototype with one task, “work through a real decision of yours,” then talked it over.

5 participants→ task in the prototype→ interview

I watched where they stalled and what they said out loud, and asked whether moving toward a decision felt easier.

What participants noted

said deciding got easier

The branch metaphor

Trunk: as things are now

The baseline; every option’s deviation is measured from it.

Branches: the options

Two is enough, a third shows up on its own. Each has a benefit, a risk and a confidence.

Confidence: facts vs guesses

It rises when guesses turn into facts, not when you just think about it longer.

How to read the map

● fact○ guess△ risk+ benefit

The decision map, enlarged: three branches, each with a confidence percentage and a “facts / guesses” counter

A reversible step
decision→ branch map→ experiment · 5 evenings→ daily note→ outcome: confidence

Instead of a “yes” or “no” – a short experiment over a few days. You come back from it with facts.

Experiment and outcome

Each evening – a short check-in: energy, interest, anxiety and a note.
